How it works

Paste one prompt. Then just tell Claude what happened.

  1. Paste the prompt and answer Claude's questions. It asks one at a time: names, sizes, what they're into, gift ideas. "Not sure" is a fine answer. Claude builds your page.
  2. Bookmark the link, then text it to the family. Grandparents, aunts, the group chat. They tap, they see everything. You're the only one who edits.
  3. When someone grabs a gift, they text you. You tell Claude. "Grandma's getting Leo the Lego set." The page updates on the same link. Nobody doubles up.
